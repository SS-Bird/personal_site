# 1780s Ensemble

This ensemble was made for my high school graduation year personal project, but it was an entirely independent project. The outfit would be appropriate for a working class woman from the 1780s. The stays are an adjusted version of the Redthreaded 1780s front lacing stays and are made with a cotton lining, two layers of mystery ‘linen’, plastic boning, leather binding and stiffened with gum tragacanthin. The rest of the ensemble was not made from a commercial patterned and made with linen.

I have worn these items fairly regularly for years now, and in retrospect these are the lessons I'd advis. 
1. If you are going to buy cheap fabric, don't buy it in a dark colour. The dark teal have bleed onto the laces and leather binding.
2. Leather shammy is not stiff enough for binding. While the strength and flexibility of the shammy has done an amazing job not fraying and never being punctured,that same flexibility means high tension areas have bonding poking out (the shammy pokes *with* the bones, the bones do not poke *through*). This might still be a good choice if you reinforce a couple places and if you trim down the bones more.
