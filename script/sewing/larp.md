# Larp and Costuming
I attended Bicolline for the first time in the summer of 2024 and have returned for the Grande Bataille every since (and the Tournament of Nations 2025). This fall I volunteered with the Brandywine festival UK. 

These events have oriented my sewing towards my own middle ground between historical and modern sewing. I still go for a most historical, rather than fantasy, look, but I have a lot of fun mixing 18th century with medival styles and adding ecclectic acessories. The kirtle pictured here is mostly historically plausible and I'd put it in the historical section, but I just don't have photos! 

Speaking of photos, the quality ones are credit to the Bicolline photography crew and my friend from Bicolline, Silk.

## A spooky dress
This was a Halloween costume inspired by vague historical bettlejuice vibes. 
The corset/stays in this dress began as a separate item. They were only the 3rd proper thing I sewed and the pattern was drafted through vibes and cardboard with a vaguely 18th century goal. They definitely have issues, but I love them.
